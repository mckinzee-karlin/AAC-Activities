Communication Functions in Real Life

Take out your cell phone and look at the last five messages you sent. What was the function of each message?

How many of each of the following types of messages did you send?

Requesting

"Can you send me that link?"   "Pick up milk on the way home."   "Call me when you get a chance."

Commenting

"That concert was amazing!"   "Traffic is terrible right now."   "I loved that episode."

Rejecting

"I can't make it tonight."   "No, that doesn't work for me."   "Please stop sending group texts."

Information

"What time does it start?"   "Where are you sitting?"   "Did you finish the report?"

Social Etiquette

"Happy Birthday!"   "Thanks for your help."   "Good morning / good night."
