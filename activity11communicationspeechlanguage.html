Activity 1.1: Communication, Speech, and Language

Fast Fact #1: AAC will not hinder speech production

What's the difference?

Before we dive in, let's define three terms that are easy to mix up.

Communication

Communication is the active process of sharing information and ideas. Communication involves both understanding and expression.

Speech

Speech is one form of communication. It is how we say sounds and use our voices.

Language

Language refers to the words we use and the rules that tell us how to use them. Language includes:

  • What words mean
  • How to make new words (friend, friendly, unfriendly)
  • How to put words together
  • How we use words socially
